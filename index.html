About the Project

Giving stories a place to be heard.

Finding Haven & Prosperity is a journalism project that explores the experiences of children living in orphanage centres, highlighting their everyday lives, challenges, dreams and opportunities.

Through interviews, photography and storytelling, the project seeks to bring attention to the realities faced by vulnerable children while also highlighting the people and organisations working to create better futures for them.
